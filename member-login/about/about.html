Welcome to Digital Library

The Digital Library is a web-based platform designed to simplify book management and member tracking. It allows librarians and students to issue, return, and monitor books efficiently — all in one place.

Our goal is to make reading and learning accessible anytime, anywhere. With features like book records, member lists, issue tracking, and detailed reports, managing your library has never been easier.

Key Features
  • 📚 Manage books and members easily
  • 🔄 Issue and return books with one click
  • 📊 View real-time reports and statistics
  • 💾 Data stored securely in local storage
